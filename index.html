01 / CREATIVE POV
REAL / CLEAR / WATCHABLE

Make it feel real.
Make the point fast.

I don’t start by asking, “How do we make an ad?” I start with the moment that would make someone stop, understand the value and keep watching.

The format can change — direct-to-camera, lifestyle, couple, pet or paid social. The thinking stays the same: believable delivery, a clear idea and no wasted seconds.

REAL PERSON+CLEAR ANGLE+RIGHT MOMENT=CONTENT THAT FITS THE FEED
01

GIVE THEM A REASON TO STAY.

The hook should feel like the start of something worth watching — not a headline pasted onto a video.

02

PUT THE PRODUCT IN A REAL MOMENT.

Context beats claims. Show where the product fits, what changes and why the viewer should care.

03

EDIT ONLY WHAT HELPS ATTENTION.

Pacing, captions, cuts and movement should make the idea clearer — never noisier.

03 / CREATOR PROFILE
THE PERSON BEHIND THE CONTENT

Not just a face
in the frame.

I’m Rostyslav — Rostyk for short — a Ukrainian UGC creator based in Italy. I create in Italian and Russian, handling the idea, filming and edit from one place.

I’m naturally hands-on: I like testing, comparing and explaining things in a simple way, especially when the product is tech, practical or part of everyday life. On camera, I keep the delivery direct and believable rather than over-scripted.

I can create solo, couple-led and pet-led content, giving brands more than one real-life angle without changing creator. The goal is simple: make the product easy to understand and the content easy to watch.
